Insurance & Cashless Health Partners

Why Choose Our Insurance Partners?

We partner with leading insurance providers to ensure that our patients enjoy a hassle-free cashless experience. Our goal is to reduce the stress of hospital visits and make healthcare accessible to everyone.

  • Cashless claims for most major policies
  • Quick approvals and minimal documentation
  • Wide network coverage across treatments
How It Works

Simply present your insurance ID card at the reception. Our insurance coordinators will assist with verification and approval, ensuring a smooth and transparent process.

  • Policy verification and eligibility check
  • Authorization for treatments covered
  • Billing directly settled with insurer
Coverage Tips

Not all treatments may be fully covered. We recommend consulting with our insurance team to understand your policy benefits and limitations.

  • Understand in-patient and out-patient coverage
  • Check limits for specific procedures
  • Clarify co-payment requirements
