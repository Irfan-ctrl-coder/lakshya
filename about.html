Our Story

Lakshya Hospital was founded with a vision to provide world-class medical services with a personal touch. Our team combines years of expertise, modern technology, and patient-focused care to create a nurturing environment for healing.

Over the years, we have expanded our specialties, partnered with trusted insurance providers, and built a network of skilled medical professionals to ensure every patient receives the highest standard of care.

Founder

Ms. Ananya Verma

Founder & Visionary Leader

Ms. Ananya Verma envisioned a hospital that combines modern medical expertise with compassionate care. Though not a doctor, her leadership and commitment have driven Lakshya Hospital to become a trusted healthcare destination.
